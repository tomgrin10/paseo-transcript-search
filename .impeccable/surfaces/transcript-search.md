# Transcript search surface

Mode: Operate. This extends the existing agents-history surface inside Paseo; native settings use the host's settings components. No replacement visual world or raster work is in scope.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Find a previous statement quickly from the keyboard, including conversations archived across every connected host. Retain the established native interface instead of introducing a separate application shell.

OWN-WORLD: Paseo semantic surface, foreground, border, accent, and status colors; platform sans typography; Lucide icons. Inherit the source's workspace/session grouping. Let Paseo render headers, settings rows, scrolling, and responsive navigation.

STORY: Open the Command Center from this host, select search, type text into the focused field, and inspect snippets labeled with their originating host. Search all online hosts concurrently; surface disconnected, missing-plugin, and failed-host coverage. Open the matching session, confirming restoration for archived sessions. Hide each plugin's sidebar entry in its native Display settings while keeping its direct surface command.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Host navigation at left on desktop; a focused full-width search field at the top of the surface, compact filters below, coverage status for all connected hosts, then matching workspace/session rows. Native Display has a single Show in sidebar switch. Narrow layouts wrap filters and use the host's compact navigation.

FORM: Code-led adaptation of an established world; no new form selection or seed applies. Preserve source visuals and host components.

FINISH: Verify real search across multiple hosts, partial coverage when a host is disconnected or lacks an updated plugin, and opening or restoring on the originating host. Verify empty results and hidden-sidebar keyboard access. Review desktop and narrow screenshots, compare the finished surface with the inherited system, and preserve attribution. No shipping raster assets are created.
